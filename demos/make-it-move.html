Our Bubble · a demo, beats 27–35

Make it move

The chapter's own questions, one at a time — and this is the one where the numbers change. Step a tick at a time, or let it play. Every tick is worked out here, in your browser, in exact halves and whole numbers.

← read the chapter

Scope. A toy, still on a napkin: four numbers on one tetrahedron and one rule for what a tick does to them, run while the page is running. Nothing here is a claim about nature.

Use ← and → to walk the beats, and the tick controls to walk the run. The whole run is in the table underneath at every step, so nothing is only visible while it is moving.

There is nothing between two ticks, and the drawing does not pretend there is: it cuts from one tick to the next with no sliding and no easing. The rule has no in-between, and a picture that invented one would be showing you something the rule does not do.

No script is loaded from anywhere, and nothing about your visit is recorded.
