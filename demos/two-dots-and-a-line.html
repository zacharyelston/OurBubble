Scope. Everything here is a toy you can draw on a napkin, and every number in it is worked out while the page is running rather than quoted from anywhere. Nothing here is a claim about nature.

Use ← and → to walk the beats. Every number is in a table as well as in the drawing, so the page reads with the pictures ignored entirely.

The drawings follow one standard: the triangle is drawn where the tetrahedron's flat unfolded net will later put it, with the same names, so nothing has to be learned twice. No colour in a drawing means anything.

No script is loaded from anywhere, and nothing about your visit is recorded.
