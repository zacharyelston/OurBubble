The napkin world, in your browser

One page per chapter of the book's first world. Each follows its chapter's questions in order, and instead of telling you the answer it hands you the object: press something, and watch what the drawing and the table do about it.

the book

Scope. These pages run a toy: a small world built inside a computer on purpose. Nothing they draw or print is a claim about nature.

    Every number on these pages comes from one engine — UniForge's napkin crate, compiled to WebAssembly, vendored in this repository and pinned by engine.lock. The pages format what it says and compute nothing, and a check refuses any number on any surface that the engine did not produce. See demos/DEMOS.md.

    No framework, no library, nothing loaded from another site, and nothing about your visit recorded.
