# PREDICTIONS — what the toy predicts, what it assumes, and what it rules out

> **The rule (from [rung 4.4](lab/warp-4-vacuum/0404-energy-number)).** A toy can only measure *ratios to a
> standard*. So a result can be a **prediction of nature** only if it is **dimensionless** (invariant under
> the arbitrary unit `E₀`), **parameter-free** (no knob tuned to the answer), and **pre-registered** (R1: the
> `assert!` committed *before* the run, so it can't have been fitted). A number that moves with your unit, or
> that you tuned to hit, is not telling you about the world.
>
> **And the north star:** *an accurate toy is still a toy; a toy that predicts is no longer a toy.* This
> ledger is the honest scoreboard for that ambition. The [FIREWALL](FIREWALL.md) is not timidity — it is
> what keeps a future real prediction **credible**: R1 is the anti-numerology guard.

## The four buckets

| bucket | meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **prediction-grade** | dimensionless + parameter-free + pre-registered, and it **matches an independent truth** (textbook physics or exact math) with **zero tuning**. A genuine (post)diction. |
| 🔵 **math / lattice-sum constant** | a dimensionless number the lattice computes *by definition* (π, ln 2, Madelung). Exact and un-tuned — but a fact of mathematics, **not** a prediction about nature. |
| ⚪ **true-by-construction** | follows from how the engine is built (an identity, an incidence relation). Not a prediction — a consistency check. |
| 🟥 **null / ruled-out** | a first-class negative (R5): a hypothesis the toy *falsifies* or bounds. |
| 🟠 **fenced hypothesis** | contested prior physics, adopted only to compute a consequence — never derived, never endorsed. |
