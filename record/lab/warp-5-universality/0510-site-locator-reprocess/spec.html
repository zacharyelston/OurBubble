# 0510 — Warp-5.10: reprocess 5.6's SITE `d_f` negative — and settle #305's ordering fork

**Chapter:** [`..`](..) · **Gate:** `core/uniforge/tests/uf5_10_site_locator_reprocess_gate.rs` ·
**Status:** REGISTERED (R1) — predictions below are committed before any run for record

Origin: issue **#306** (opened by rung 5.9 as its own deferred item), which asks the obvious
question 5.9 raised and could not answer: rung 5.6's `d_f` negative has the *same shape* as 5.7's,
and 5.6 never computed its locator's standard error either — **does it dissolve too?** This rung also
carries the decisive extra evidence for issue **#305** (the estimator-ordering fork), because site
percolation has **two** answer keys — one of them exact — where 5.9 had only one.

## Goal (one paragraph)

Rung 5.6 registered a first-class negative: on the stella tet-oct graph under **site** dilution,
`d_f` at the crossing-located threshold came out `2.43782`, missing its registered band
`2.52295 ± 0.06`, and the miss was locked with a post-hoc diagnosis (`∂d_f/∂p ≈ 117`; `d_f = 2.52498`
at its pre-registered constant `0.1752`). Rung 5.9 showed for **bond** that such a miss can be
entirely the locator, once the locator's own error is quantified and propagated. This rung applies
the same two-outcome design to 5.6 — with a stronger control set than 5.9 had, because 2-D site
percolation has an **exact** threshold (`1/2`) *and* an **exact** class exponent (`91/48`), so the
zero-location-error control has no literature uncertainty at either end. **Both outcomes are results
and both are written down below before the run.**

## Firewall (R3)

*Percolation, cluster, threshold, wrapping, fractal dimension* name properties of site-diluted
**toy graphs**: a vertex is "occupied" with probability `p` under a seeded RNG, clusters are
connected components of the induced subgraph, `R_L(p)` is the probability a component winds a
periodic torus, and `d_f` is the fitted exponent of `s_max(L) ~ L^{d_f}`. `p_c(stella-site)` is **a
constant of this complex** (bucket 🔵): exact mathematics of a toy graph, never a property of matter.
`d_f` is a dimensionless universal invariant of the percolation class, shared with real disordered
media — this rung claims **class-membership resolution, computed**, and (where it lands) that a
*previous* class-membership miss was an artefact of **our own estimator**, not a statement about
nature. Toy DEC computation; `c = G = 1`, dimensionless throughout.

## Answer keys (independent of anything this repo computed)

- **2-D, EXACT at both ends — the strongest control in this chapter.** Triangular-site
  `p_c = 1/2` exactly (Sykes & Essam 1964; rigorous: Kesten 1980/82), and the 2-D percolation class
  is exactly solved: `d_f = 91/48 = 1.8958333…` (den Nijs 1979; SLE₆). Measuring `d_f` *at* `1/2`
  removes the locator from the experiment with **no literature uncertainty anywhere**.
- **3-D, published.** FCC-site `p_c = 0.1992365(10)` — Lorenz & Ziff, J. Stat. Phys. **98**, 961
  (2000). Class exponent `β/ν = 0.47705(15)` ⇒ `d_f = 3 − β/ν = 2.52295(15)` — Wang, Zhou, Zhang,
  Garoni, Deng, Phys. Rev. E **87**, 052107 (2013).

## Method (sketch)

The percolation machinery — `Rng` (splitmix64), the triangular / FCC / stella torus edge lists, the
union-find-with-displacement wrapping detector, `sample`/`measure`, `sweep_batch`, `crossing_estimate`
and the `measure_at` seed scheme (`SEED_MEAS ^ (l<<40) ^ (rep<<2)`, **independent of `p`**, so two
`p`-points are a *paired* comparison) — is **copied verbatim** from
`uf5_6_stella_percolation_gate.rs`. Copy fidelity is not assumed; it is P0a/P0b. The locator is
`kinematics::crossing_extrapolate` (5.8's tool) with `p_c_stderr` (added by 5.9).

Registered grids, stated explicitly per the 5.6 lesson — **5.6's own windows, unchanged**:
triangular `p ∈ [0.485, 0.515]` (7 pts, step 0.005), `L ∈ {16,24,32,48,64,96}` ⇒ 5 pair crossings;
FCC-site `p ∈ [0.193, 0.205]` (7 pts, step 0.002) and stella-site `p ∈ [0.170, 0.184]` (8 pts, step
0.002), both `L ∈ {12,18,24,36,48}` ⇒ 4 pair crossings; fixed class drift exponent `w = 2`
(`= 1/ν + θ`). FSS measurement `L ∈ {18,24,36,48}` (3-D) / `{24,32,48,64,96}` (2-D), `R = 2400`,
largest three sizes fitted (the 5.1 rule); `d_f`'s own statistical error from the 4 independent seed
stripes (`kinematics::replica_stats` — the 5.5 rule). Paired central difference for `∂d_f/∂p`:
`±0.0006` in 3-D, `±0.005` in 2-D (the 2-D amplification is ~10× gentler, so the step must be ~10×
wider to clear the statistical noise — probed).

Skipped deliberately: the engine-mesh-`d0`-vs-rule graph identity walk (5.6's own P2a proved it at
365 vertices and the graph is unchanged); reproducing 5.6's committed batch estimates *and* both of
its committed `d_f` values is a strictly stronger check that this is the same machinery.

## Predictions (registered before the run)

Every threshold was measured first by the committed probe
(`core/uniforge/examples/uf5_10_site_locator_probe.rs`; full log in
[`data/probe_log.txt`](data/probe_log.txt)). The re-analysis arms are deterministic functions of
committed data and the MC arms are seeded, so the gate *reproduces* these numbers rather than
discovering them — stated plainly.

### P0 — controls. If any fails, nothing about stella can be claimed.

- **P0a (sweep copy fidelity, all three families):** re-running 5.6's sweeps with 5.6's seeds
  reproduces its committed per-batch naive estimator to `1e-6` (the paper trail's own 6-decimal
  resolution) in both mean and `σ_mean`: triangular `0.500986 / 0.001348`, FCC `0.199457 / 0.000296`,
  stella `0.174453 / 0.000410`. *Probed: reproduced at all 6 decimals, batch by batch.*
- **P0b (measurement copy fidelity, both of 5.6's quoted `d_f` values):**
  `|d_f(stella, 0.174453) − 2.43782| < 1e-3` *(probed `1.15e-4`)* **and**
  `|d_f(stella, 0.1752) − 2.52498| < 1e-3` *(probed `7.2e-7` — 5.6's pre-registered constant is
  exact in the paper trail, so this arm is essentially bit-exact; the located-`p_c` arm's larger
  residue is `113 ×` the 6-decimal rounding of the committed threshold)*.
- **P0c (the EXACT zero-location-error control — no literature uncertainty at either end):**
  triangular-site at **exactly** `p = 1/2`, `|d_f − 91/48| < 0.02`. *Probed `1.89613 ± 0.00722`,
  `|Δ| = 3.0e-4` = **0.04 σ_stat**.* This is the arm that decides "is `d_f` broken, or is it the
  locator?", and here it is decided against an exactly known answer.
- **P0d (the published zero-location-error control, 3-D):** FCC-site at `0.1992365`,
  `|d_f − 2.52295| < 0.05`. *Probed `2.50814 ± 0.01334`, `|Δ| = 0.0148` (1.1 σ_stat) — measured
  slightly **low**, the corrections-to-scaling direction 5.6 pre-registered as **high**; registered
  as a two-sided bound and discussed in the eval rather than smoothed over.*

### P1 — the locator and its error bar, calibrated against TWO keys

- **P1a:** `|p̂_c(stella-site, aggregate, w=2) − 0.174911| < 1e-5`, and it sits **above** 5.6's
  located `0.174453` (registered direction; probed gap `+0.00046`). This is 5.8's own committed
  value, recomputed here — 5.8 located it and never re-measured `d_f` there.
- **P1b (the fit is honest about itself):** `p_c_stderr(stella) ∈ [3.0e-4, 8.0e-4]` *(probed
  `5.14e-4`)*, and `r² < 0.50` for **all three** families *(probed `0.2424 / 0.4050 / 0.1758`)* — the
  drift is weakly resolved at best, so this locator is not being sold as a better fit.
- **P1c (error-bar calibration on both keys):** for **triangular** and **FCC** independently,
  `|p̂_c − key| < 1.5 × p_c_stderr` **and** `|p̂_c − key| > 2.0 × (w∈[1.6,2.4] spread)`.
  *Probed: triangular `0.81 × se`, `5.28 × spread`; FCC `0.55 × se`, `2.25 × spread`.* This is 5.9's

### P2 — the systematics that set the band (probed BEFORE the band, per the 5.6/5.7/5.9 lesson)

- **P2a (verify the amplification, do not assume it):** paired central difference at the new locator,

- **P2b (the ordering systematic):** `|p̂_c(aggregate-then-extrapolate) − p̂_c(extrapolate-then-average)|`

- **P2c (#305's answer — the fork has NO universal licence, and this is the falsifiable form):** on

  `|per-batch − key| < |aggregate − key|` for triangular **and** FCC.

- **P3a (reproducibility):** `|d_f(stella, p̂_c) − 2.49050| < 0.01`.
- **P3b (the improvement claim — falsifiable and directional):**
  `|d_f(p̂_c) − 2.52295| < 0.5 × 0.08513 = 0.0426` **and** `d_f(p̂_c) > 2.43782` (upward, the sign the

- **P3c (dissolve or certify — evaluated at ALL THREE candidate locators, each with its own quoted

  per-batch-extrapolated locator, and the aggregate-extrapolated locator,
  `z = |d_f − 2.52295| / √((sens × loc_err)² + σ_stat²) < 2`.
  - **Registered branch — H-DISSOLVE:** 5.6's `d_f` miss was **the locator's error, unpropagated**.
    Its registered scope-statement stands verbatim (its windows, sizes, numbers and its locked
    diagnosis all held and are reproduced here) while its *interpretation* is upgraded — written into
    5.6's own `eval.md` as a dated marked note, never silently. *Probed `z = 1.69 / 1.73 / 0.52`.*
    Note this is a **stronger** dissolve than 5.9's: there, the two orderings gave opposite verdicts;
    here every candidate locator dissolves, including 5.6's own.
  - **Pre-registered alternative — H-CERTIFY (`z ≥ 2` at the licensed locator):** the miss survives a
    propagated band, which would be a genuine finding about **this family and this graph at
    `L ≤ 48`** (never about nature), and the next discriminator is named in advance: `L` up to

  - *gate:* assert the registered branch for all three locators. If the run returns the alternative,

### P4 — the inverted locator, now with an EXACT key at both ends

- **P4a (2-D, exact):** anchoring `d_f` at the exact class value and dividing by the measured local
  sensitivity, `p_c(d_f-anchored) = 1/2 + (91/48 − d_f(1/2))/sens_tri`, recovers the **exact**

- **P4b (3-D, published):** the same inversion on FCC-site recovers `0.1992365` to `|Δ| < 3e-4`.

  *mechanism* rather than as independent evidence.
- **Bucket honesty (unchanged from 5.9):** the inversion consumes the literature (or exact) class
  exponent as an input, so it is a 🔵 method/cross-check and can **never** be 🟢 prediction-grade.

**Report-only (written to `data/`, not gated):** the full 6-point `d_f`-vs-locator ladder; the

## What would falsify this

- **P0a/P0b failing** ⇒ the copied machinery is not 5.6's; every number is void and the rung reports
  an inconclusive re-analysis (R5), not a verdict on 5.6.
- **P0c failing** ⇒ `d_f` is not sound at these sizes even against an **exactly** known threshold and
  an **exactly** known exponent. Then no locator story survives and the follow-on is an `L`-ladder on
  the FSS fit itself, not another estimator. This is the arm most able to kill the rung.
- **P0d failing with P0c green** ⇒ the 3-D FSS fit carries a corrections-to-scaling bias larger than
  registered; the dissolve claim would then need that bias in the band too, and the eval must say so.
- **P1c failing** ⇒ either the standard error does not cover the real error (the band is untrustworthy
  and no dissolve claim can be made) or the `w`-spread is a fair error bar after all (5.9's
  methodological correction is withdrawn). Reported either way.
- **P2c failing** ⇒ the site keys agree with the bond key, the aggregate ordering *is* universally
  licensed, and 5.9's plank was fine — a correction **to this rung**, reported as such.
- **P2d failing** ⇒ 5.6's registered `± 0.06` was in fact wide enough for its own locator error, and
  the "it was inside an error bar you could have computed" framing is wrong.
- **P3b failing with P0–P2 green** ⇒ the better-centred locator does not move `d_f` toward the class
  value: a first-class negative about the locator-error explanation itself.
- **P3c returning `z ≥ 2`** at the licensed locator ⇒ H-CERTIFY, above. Not a failure — the other
  registered outcome.
- **P4a failing** ⇒ the inverted locator is not licensed even against an exact key; it is withdrawn
  (reported, not widened) and the derived stella number is deleted rather than quoted.

## Determinism

base `0x5EED ⊕ k·2⁵⁰`, measurement base `0xABCD`, `K=5` batches × `R=300` sweep samples, `R=2400`
measurement samples, fixed 4-way thread striping so accumulation order is independent of core count).
The re-analysis arms read a committed CSV and are closed-form least squares. Pure `f64`, no
wall-clock, no unseeded RNG — the same numbers on every machine, every run. Probe runtime 24 s; the
gate is budgeted at ~25–30 s (tier 2).
