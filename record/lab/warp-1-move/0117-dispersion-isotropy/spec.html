# 0117 — Warp-1.7: the bench discovers `ω = c·k` blind, in five directions at once

**Chapter:** [`..`](..) · **Gate:** `core/uniforge/tests/uf1_7_dispersion_isotropy_gate.rs` ·
**Status:** **CONFIRMED (P0–P4, first run)** — see [`eval.md`](eval.md). The predictions below were

## Goal (one paragraph)

Rung 1.6/1.6b pointed the discovery bench at the lattice light cone **along z only** and let
`kinematics::power_law_fit` recover the exponent. #46's dispersion task asks for the full statement:
`ω = c·k`, discovered blind, **and isotropic**. This rung supplies the two missing halves. (a) The
sweep runs on a **fully periodic 3-torus** (`mesh_3d_tetrahedral_grid_periodic`, seam-correct
`unfolded_scalar_stars`) so there is no open boundary anywhere and plane waves along `[100]`, `[010]`,
`[001]`, `[110]` and `[111]` are all legal fields — five directions, one operator. (b) The law is
chosen by a new **answer-free model selector**, `kinematics::select_power_law`, which scores a fixed
menu of candidate laws `{k⁰, k^½, k¹, k^{3/2}, k², k³}` by log-space `R²` and returns whichever the
data prefers. Neither the selector nor `power_law_fit` has a parameter through which the expected
exponent could enter — the gate reads the *result* and asserts on it. Alongside the discovery, two
pre-registered honesty arms: the finite lattice's **short-wavelength bend-down** (1.6's lesson), and a
**negative control** — the same pipeline run on the trivial `⋆ = I` operator, which must come back
strongly *anisotropic*, or "isotropic" would be an unfalsifiable word.

## Prior known physics, stated up front (R3 / #46's "validated re-derivation" arm)

This is a **discovery reframing of known numerics, not new physics**, and the probe made that
sharper than expected. On this mesh the geometric ⋆ assigns `⋆₁ = 1` to every axis edge and
**exactly `0`** to every face- and body-diagonal edge (`⋆₀ = 1` uniformly) — the 3-D version of
rung 3.5's "`cot 90° = 0` kills the diagonals". So `Δ₀ = ⋆₀⁻¹D₀ᵀ⋆₁D₀` here **is** the textbook
7-point simple-cubic stencil, whose symbol `λ(k) = Σ_a (2 − 2cos k_a)` is closed-form. The sweep
therefore has an exact analytic answer key, and P0 checks the machinery against it. That is the
point of this arm of #46: the bench must first be shown re-deriving physics whose answer is already
known, blind, before any blind claim about something unknown is worth anything. What is *not*
known in advance to the analysis path is the law itself — the code that turns `(|k|, ω)` into
"`ω ∝ k¹`, `R² = 0.9999`" never receives it.

## Firewall (R3)

A **toy** DEC scalar wave on a tetrahedral 3-torus. `ω`, `k`, "dispersion", "light cone",
"lightlike", "speed", "isotropy" name **numerical properties of the discrete operator**
`Δ₀ = ⋆₀⁻¹D₀ᵀ⋆₁D₀` and of least-squares fits to its Rayleigh quotients — never a real photon, a real
light cone, or a measurement of `c`. The **prediction-grade** quantities are the dimensionless,
parameter-free **exponent** and the dimensionless **anisotropy ratio** (rung-4.4 criterion); the
fitted coefficient is an *emergent lattice wave speed* in code units, reported but **not** claimed to
be any physical constant. `c = 1` in code units only. Nothing here is a claim about nature.

## Method (sketch)

`geom::mesh::mesh_3d_tetrahedral_grid_periodic(64,64,64)` (262 144 vertices, 1 572 864 tets, unit
spacing, no boundary) → `solve::bloch::unfolded_scalar_stars` with `Lattice::orthorhombic([64,64,64])`
(minimum-image element assembly — raw glued coordinates are seam-corrupt on *every* axis of a
3-torus, #177/#153-Stage-2) → for each direction `n ∈ {[100],[010],[001],[110],[111]}` and each
`m = 1,2,…`, the exactly-commensurate plane wave `φ(x) = cos(k·x)` with `k = (2πm/64)·n`, and the
Rayleigh quotient `λ = ⟨φ,Δ₀φ⟩/⟨φ,φ⟩` via `dec::operators::apply_laplacian_0_metric`, `ω = √λ` →
`kinematics::{select_power_law, power_law_fit}` on `(|k|, ω)`.

Windows (registered): **long-λ** `|k| ≤ K = 1.05` (which sits between `π/3` and the next `[100]`
mode, so every direction gets ≥ 6 modes); **half** `|k| ≤ K/2 = 0.525` (≥ 3 modes in every
direction); **full range** `|k| ≤ 2K = 2.10`. Anisotropy of the discovered speed is
`A = (C_max − C_min)/C̄` over the five directions. Fully deterministic (pure f64, no RNG, no
wall-clock). Probe: `core/uniforge/examples/dispersion_isotropy_probe.rs` (committed breadcrumb;
also carries the `N ∈ {48,64,72}` ladder and a raw-vs-unfolded star arm not registered here).

  - `⋆₀` is uniform: `max|⋆₀ − 1| < 1e-12`. *(probe: 4.4e-16.)*
  - `⋆₁ = 1` on **every** axis edge (`max|⋆₁ − 1| < 1e-12`; probe 1.1e-16) and **exactly 0** on

  - therefore the operator is the 7-point simple-cubic stencil:
    `max|λ_measured − Σ_a(2 − 2cos k_a)| < 1e-9` over every probed mode. *(probe: 8.23e-12; `λ`
    ranges to ~4, so this is ~2e-12 relative — 7.0's "bound the residue relative to what it rides
    on".)*
  - the three axis directions, related by a cubic symmetry, agree to `< 1e-9` relative.
    *(probe: 7.44e-13.)*

## What would falsify this

If `select_power_law` returned anything but the linear law in any direction, or the free exponent
left `1.00 ± 0.03`, the lattice wave would not be lightlike at long wavelength and the "discovered
light cone" story fails. If `A(K) ≥ 0.03`, the cone is not isotropic at the registered resolution.
If `A(K)/A(K/2)` came back near 1 instead of ~2.6, the residual anisotropy would be a genuine
**leading-order** cone defect surviving the geometric ⋆ — a real negative, and the interesting one:
it would say the isotropy of rung 1.5 does not survive to the discovered *law*. If `p_full ≥ p_long`
in any direction the lattice would show no discretization signature, meaning the fit is measuring an
artifact rather than the operator. And if the `⋆ = I` control came back *isotropic* (P4), the whole
isotropy measurement would be vacuous — a detector that cannot fail is not a detector, and the rung
would be withdrawn rather than reported.

## Scope of the negatives / controls (registered)

P4's statement is about **this** mesh (the Kuhn 3-torus at `N = 64`) and **this** trivial star
(`⋆₀ = ⋆₁ = 1`), on the two registered windows. P0's "`⋆₁ = 0` on every diagonal" is a census of
this mesh's own unfolded geometric star and nothing wider. In particular, the exact zeros on 2/3 of
the edges mean the operator is **blind to diagonal edges**; that is benign for the 0-form cone
measured here, and 8.15 already showed a zero ⋆ opening a spurious null space in the 1-form sector —
this rung makes **no** claim about 1-forms/photons on this mesh.
