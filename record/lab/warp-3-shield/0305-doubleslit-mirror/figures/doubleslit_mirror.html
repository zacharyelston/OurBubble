The double-slit demo's lopsided fringes — cause and exact repair

The public demo's "two-slit" pattern is 3.8× brighter on one side of a perfectly symmetric scene. The cause is the mesh, not the wave: every grid cell's diagonal leans the same way. Alternate the diagonals and the pattern is mirror-symmetric to machine precision.

FIREWALL. Toy DEC scalar wave on a 200×140 triangle lattice (the demo's exact scene). Slit, screen, fringe, intensity name lattice features and derived observables, never claims about nature. Not a Maxwell/EM simulation; no claim about physical double-slit experiments.
