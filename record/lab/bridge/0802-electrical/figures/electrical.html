The 4th view — electricity & random walks

The same graph Laplacian, read as a resistor network and as a random walk. The two agree through a famous duality (commute time = 2E·resistance), which is how this view *earns its place*. Its flagship number is the stella spanning-tree entropy.

The spanning-tree entropy constant
