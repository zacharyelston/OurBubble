The automated bench — one law-finder, many laws

The lattice is a cheap virtual workbench; the value-add is automation. One reusable fitter (kinematics::power_law_fit) discovers scaling laws across unrelated experiments and checks each against theory.

FIREWALL. Pure data-analysis of toy-lattice outputs — a log–log power-law fit and the periodic graph-Laplacian heat-kernel trace. The "dimension" is the toy lattice's spectral dimension, not a claim about nature. c = 1.

Measuring the lattice's dimension from its heat kernel

The heat-kernel trace decays as K(t) ∝ t^(−d/2). On log–log axes each dimension is a straight line of slope −d/2 — the fitter reads the dimension straight off the slope.

d=1 (slope −½) d=2 (slope −1) d=3 (slope −3/2)

What the one fitter discovered — across three experiments

The same power_law_fit recovers a synthetic law, the Casimir force scaling, and three lattice dimensions — three different exponents, all held against theory.
